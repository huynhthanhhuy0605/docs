Qrdy makes **focused tools that do one thing well** — mostly Canva apps for creators, plus a family craft web app and a calm puzzle game. Instead of one bloated product, each Qrdy app solves a specific job: turn an image into a frame, fill text with photos, generate a calendar, and more. This page walks through every tool we focus on and how to use it, step by step.

## The Qrdy tools at a glance

| Tool | What it does | Where it runs |
|---|---|---|
| **Image to Frames** | Turns any image into a custom Canva frame | Canva app |
| **Text to Frames** | Turns each letter into its own frame to fill with photos | Canva app |
| **Frames Lab** | A toolbox to build frames (grid, arc, burst, diagonal…) | Canva app |
| **Calendar Maker** | Generates month / week / custom-range calendars | Canva app |
| **QrdyCraft** | Turns a photo or keyword into a mosaic sticker game | Web + Canva |
| **Hepta** | A calm, ad-free merge puzzle game | Mobile (iOS) |

The first four are part of our **Frames & design** family for Canva. QrdyCraft and Hepta are standalone experiences. Let's go through each.

## Frames & design (Canva apps)

### Image to Frames — turn any image into a frame

A Canva frame crops whatever photo you drop into it. Canva's built-in frames are basic shapes; **Image to Frames** converts *any* image (PNG, JPG, or SVG) into a valid Canva frame.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Image to Frames">
In Canva, open **Apps** and launch Image to Frames.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick an image">
Use an image from your design, upload a file, or import one from the frame library.
</Step>
<Step title="Convert">
Click convert — your image becomes a frame in one click, fully automatic.
</Step>
<Step title="Drop a photo in">
Drag any photo into the new frame; it crops to the shape instantly.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Text to Frames — fill your text with photos

**Text to Frames** turns **each letter of a word into its own Canva frame**, so you can drop a different photo or video into every letter — something a single clipping mask can't do.

<Steps>
<Step title="Type your text">
Open the app, type a word, and pick from 100+ fonts.
</Step>
<Step title="Split into letters">
Turn on **Split into letters** so each character becomes a separate frame.
</Step>
<Step title="Add effects (optional)">
Add **Blur**, **Border**, or **Inner shadow**, each with its own color.
</Step>
<Step title="Add to design and fill">
Click **Add to design**, then drag a photo into each letter.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Frames Lab — a full frame-building toolbox

When you need a frame built to spec, **Frames Lab** gives you five create modes plus ready-made collections and styling effects.

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose a Create mode">
Pick **Shape, Burst, Grid, Arc, or Diagonal** — or open **Explore** for ready-made frames.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the parameters">
Adjust the controls (grid columns/rows, arc sweep angle, burst density…) with a live preview.
</Step>
<Step title="Add to design">
Insert the frame — it auto-fits your design size.
</Step>
<Step title="Style it (optional)">
In the **Style** tab, re-add a frame with blur, border, or inner shadow.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Calendar Maker — generate calendars in seconds

**Calendar Maker** builds an accurate calendar grid so you never draw a table by hand. Choose a month, a week layout, or any custom date range.

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick a calendar type">
Open the app and choose the **Month**, **Week**, or **Custom** tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the dates">
Enter a month and year, choose weekdays, or set a start/end date.
</Step>
<Step title="Style it">
Adjust fonts and colors, or apply one of 30+ preset color themes; use a page template for a decorated look.
</Step>
<Step title="Add to design">
Click **Add to design** and the calendar drops into your Canva page.
</Step>
</Steps>

## QrdyCraft — a mosaic sticker game from any photo

**QrdyCraft** turns a photo or keyword into a **mosaic sticker-by-number**. It's **parent-operated and kid-played**: you set it up, your child (ages 4–12) finishes it — on screen or printed.

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a photo or keyword">
At [qrdyai.com/craft](https://qrdyai.com/craft), upload a picture or type a keyword.
</Step>
<Step title="Generate the mosaic">
AI builds a numbered, colored mosaic in a few seconds.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose how to play">
Play online by dragging numbered stickers, or download a print-ready PDF to stick by number at home.
</Step>
<Step title="Complete the picture">
Match each numbered piece to reveal the finished full-color art.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Hepta — a calm merge puzzle

**Hepta** is a relaxing, **ad-free** merge puzzle. Merge two pieces that share the same color and shape, evolving through seven shapes from a circle to a heptagon, and build the target piece to clear each level.

<Steps>
<Step title="Install Hepta">
Get Hepta from the App Store and open it — every level is bundled, so it plays offline.
</Step>
<Step title="Merge matching pieces">
Combine two pieces of the same color and shape to make the next shape up.
</Step>
<Step title="Build the target">
Each level shows a target piece at the top — build it to clear the level.
</Step>
<Step title="Relax across 150 levels">
Work through 150 hand-crafted, always-solvable levels — no ads, no pay-to-win.
</Step>
</Steps>
