Hepta is built to be calm and fair. The whole game is yours for free, and the optional Unlimited passes only change one thing: how long you can play without worrying about hearts.

## Free to play, no ads

Hepta is **free to play**, with **no ads, ever** — no banners and no pop-ups. There are also **no coins, no boosters, and no shop**. You won't be nudged to spend to keep going, and nothing interrupts a level. It's just you, the board, and a quiet puzzle.

## What Unlimited gives you

Unlimited is a single, simple option for players who want to keep going without pauses.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
<Card title="Heart-free play" icon="infinity">
An Unlimited pass lets you play **heart-free for a set amount of time** — failing a level won't spend a heart while it's active.
</Card>

<Card title="Short timed passes" icon="clock">
Passes come as **short, timed durations**, so you can choose a stretch of uninterrupted play that suits you.
</Card>
</CardGroup>

When a pass is active, the heart limit simply doesn't apply. When it ends, everything returns to normal and your hearts work as usual again.

## Never pay-to-win

Unlimited is fair by design. Hepta **never sells solutions** and **never makes levels harder** to push you toward a purchase.

<Note>
Every one of Hepta's 150 levels is hand-crafted and verified solvable, whether or not you ever buy a pass. Unlimited only lifts the heart limit for a while — it never gives you an advantage on the board.
</Note>

That's the point of the model: the puzzle stays exactly as designed for everyone, easy to learn and hard to master, and spending never changes the challenge.

## How to get Unlimited

Unlimited passes are purchased through the **App Store** on iPhone and iPad, or **Google Play** on Android. You can start a pass from within Hepta, and the store handles the purchase securely using your existing account.

## Restore Purchases

If a purchase isn't recognized — for example after reinstalling Hepta or switching to a new device — use **Restore Purchases**. This checks your store account and reapplies a pass you've already bought, so you don't pay again.

<Tip>
Make sure you're signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you used originally, then run Restore Purchases.
</Tip>
