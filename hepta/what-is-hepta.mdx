Hepta is a relaxing merge puzzle that is easy to learn and hard to master. You merge two pieces that share the **same color and the same shape**, and they combine into the next piece up. Step by step your pieces evolve through seven shapes, from a soft **circle** all the way to a glowing **heptagon**.

Each level shows a **target piece** at the top of the board. Your goal is simple and satisfying: build that target piece to clear the level. Low levels look like gentle candy, and as you climb they start to look like polished gems.
