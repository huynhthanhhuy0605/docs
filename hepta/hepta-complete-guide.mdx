## How to play

<img src="/images/hepta/gameplay.png" alt="A Hepta level in play with the board grid, target piece at the top, and colored pieces" style={{maxHeight: '50vh', width: 'auto', height: 'auto', objectFit: 'contain'}} />

The loop is simple: find two pieces matching in **both color and shape**, drag one onto the other to evolve up a shape, and repeat toward the **target piece** at the top. Build the target to clear the level.

## Merging, direction & scoring

Merging is where the depth lives:

- **Direction matters.** When two pieces merge, the tile you **drop ONTO keeps its score**. Dragging A onto B isn't the same as B onto A — decide which tile to keep before you merge.
- **The ripple.** Each merge **ripples to the 4 neighboring tiles** (up, down, left, right), scoring points on them. One well-placed merge can score across several tiles.
- **Stones.** Stones are obstacles with no special button — **break a stone by merging on a tile next to it**, and the ripple cracks it.
- **Stars.** Solving in **fewer moves earns more stars** (1–3). Your best move count per level is saved so you can come back and beat it.

<Tip>
To protect a tile's points, make it the one you **drop onto**, not the one you drag away.
</Tip>

## Levels, stars & the map

<Frame>
<img src="/images/hepta/levels.png" alt="The Hepta level map showing 150 levels along a winding path with their states and stars" style={{maxHeight: '50vh', width: 'auto', height: 'auto', objectFit: 'contain'}} />

Hepta has **150 hand-crafted levels** on a winding map. Level 1 is open from the start; clear a level to unlock the next. Each level shows its state — **Locked, Current, Open, or Cleared** (with stars earned). Every level rewards **1–3 stars** based on how few moves you use, and all 150 are verified solvable.

<Info>
Three stars are about solving smart, not fast. There's no timer — take your time and plan each move.
</Info>

When you're stuck, tap the **Hint** button to highlight the next good move. Use it as often as you like.

## Hearts (your play energy)

Hearts pace your play and are designed to stay out of the way:

- You **lose a heart only when you fail** a level — never when you win.
- **Tutorial levels never cost a heart.**
- Hearts **refill over time** in the background while you're away — nothing to claim.
- Out of hearts? **Wait a short while** for them to refill, or get an optional **Unlimited play** pass to lift the limit for a while.
