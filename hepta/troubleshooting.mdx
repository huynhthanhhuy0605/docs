---

Running into a snag? Most things are quick to sort out. Hepta is a calm merge puzzle, and these friendly fixes will get you back to playing in no time.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I'm out of hearts">
    No problem — you only lose a heart when you fail a level, never when you win. Wait a short while for your hearts to refill over time, or get an Unlimited pass for heart-free play for a set amount of time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm stuck on a level">
    Don't worry — every level is verified solvable, so there's always a way through. Tap the **Hint** button to highlight the next good move. Remember that direction matters when you merge, so try solving in fewer moves to find a cleaner path.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My purchase didn't go through">
    If a purchase isn't recognized, use **Restore Purchases**. This re-checks your App Store or Google Play account and restores anything you've already bought.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The game won't open or load">
    Hepta plays fully offline, so a connection isn't needed to load your levels. If the game won't open, simply close and reopen the app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Sound or haptics aren't working">
    Open **Settings** and check that **Sound** and **Haptics** (vibration) are turned on. You can toggle each one independently.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I want to see the tutorial again">
    You can replay the **How to Play** tutorial anytime from **Settings**. It walks you through merging and the basics whenever you'd like a refresher.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
