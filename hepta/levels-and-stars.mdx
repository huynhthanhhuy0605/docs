Hepta gives you **150 hand-crafted levels** to enjoy at your own pace. They sit on a winding map and open up one at a time, so there is always a clear next step and never any pressure to rush.

## The level map

Your levels live on a single **winding map**. They **unlock one by one**: clear a level to open the next one along the path. **Level 1 is open from the start**, so you can jump in right away.

Because Hepta is easy to learn and hard to master, the map gives you a gentle sense of progress. Each cleared level lights your way to the one after it, and you can always look back to see how far you have come.

## Level states

On the map, each level shows its current state at a glance.

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Locked** | Not open yet. Clear the level before it to unlock this one. |
| **Current** | The level you are up to next on the path. |
| **Open** | Available to play whenever you like. |
| **Cleared** | Already solved, shown with the stars you earned. |

## Earning stars

Every level rewards you with **1 to 3 stars** based on how few moves you use to solve it. Clearing a level is always enough to move on, and solving it efficiently is how you reach the full three stars.

Your **best move count** for each level is saved, so you can come back later and try to beat it. There is no timer and no rush, just a quiet goal to chase when you feel like it.

<Info>
Three stars are about solving smart, not solving fast. Take your time and plan each move.
</Info>

## Every level is solvable

All **150 levels are verified solvable**. You will never get stuck on an impossible board, so if a level feels tricky, there is always a way through. That is part of what keeps Hepta calm: the challenge is fair, and the solution is always there for you to find.

## Stuck? Use a hint

When you are not sure what to do next, tap the **Hint** button. It highlights the **next good move** to help you keep going. Use it as often as you like, whether you want a small nudge or a fresh look at the board.
