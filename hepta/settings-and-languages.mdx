Hepta keeps its options simple. From the Settings screen you can turn sound and vibration on or off, pick a theme, choose your language, and reopen the tutorial anytime.

## Opening Settings

Open Settings to adjust how Hepta looks, sounds, and feels. Everything here is optional, so feel free to set it up the way that suits you best.

## Sound & Haptics

You can turn the game's audio and vibration on or off:

- **Sound** — toggle audio effects on or off.
- **Haptics** — toggle vibration on or off.

<Tip>
Prefer a quiet, distraction-free session? Switch both off and enjoy Hepta in calm silence.
</Tip>

## Theme

Choose how Hepta looks:

- **System** — follow your device's appearance setting.
- **Light** — a bright theme.
- **Dark** — a darker theme.

## Language

Hepta is available in **19 languages**. The game auto-selects a language for you, and you can switch to another one manually anytime from Settings.
