Students and teachers can use **Calendar Maker for free** with the **Qrdy Edu Pack**. Once you have your code, redeeming it takes about a minute: open the app in Canva, click **Unlock to use**, and apply the code at checkout.

<Note>
Don't have a code yet? On the Edu Pack page (Step 2), click **Get the free code** and submit the quick form with your school email. We'll send your code for Calendar Maker, plus a few other Qrdy tools.
</Note>

## Redeem your student code in 3 steps

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Calendar Maker and click “Unlock to use”">
On your code card, click **Open in Canva & Use Code** — or open Canva, go to **Apps** in the left sidebar, search for **Calendar Maker**, and open it.

In the app panel, scroll to the bottom and click **Unlock to use**.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/calendar-maker/student/calendar-maker-unlock-to-use-canva-app.png" alt="Calendar Maker app panel in Canva with the Unlock to use button highlighted" style={{maxHeight: '50vh', width: 'auto', height: 'auto', objectFit: 'contain'}} />
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Click “Already have a code?”">
On the unlock page, **scroll down to near the bottom of the page** — the **Free · Qrdy Edu Pack** section (*Student or teacher? You don't pay.*) sits below the pricing plans. Click **Already have a code?**

<Tip>
Don't see it at first? Keep scrolling — the Edu Pack section is near the end of the page, not at the top.
</Tip>

<Frame>
<img src="/images/calendar-maker/student/calendar-maker-free-edu-pack-students-teachers.png" alt="Qrdy Edu Pack section for Calendar Maker with the Already have a code button highlighted" />
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Enter your code at checkout">
On the checkout page, scroll to the bottom, paste your code into the **Discount code** field, and click **Apply**. The total drops to $0 — finish checkout and Calendar Maker is unlocked.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/calendar-maker/student/calendar-maker-student-discount-code-checkout.png" alt="Checkout page with the student code entered in the Discount code field and the Apply button highlighted" style={{maxHeight: '50vh', width: 'auto', height: 'auto', objectFit: 'contain'}} />
</Frame>

<Check>
Done! Go back to Canva, click **Restart app** in the Calendar Maker panel, and start making calendars.
</Check>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="My code says it's invalid or expired">
Codes have an expiry date. Copy the code again to avoid typos or extra spaces. If it has expired, click **Get the free code** on the Edu Pack page to request a new one.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The total didn't drop to $0">
Make sure you clicked **Apply** after pasting the code. The discount only shows after it's applied.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Calendar Maker is still locked after checkout">
Click **Restart app** at the bottom of the app panel. If it's still locked, close and reopen your Canva design.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who is eligible for the Qrdy Edu Pack?">
Students and teachers. Submit the form with your school email to get your code.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
