Calendar Maker is a Canva app that builds a ready-to-edit calendar in seconds. Enter a month and year — or any custom date range — and it generates the calendar grid for you, so you can skip the manual table work and go straight to designing.

## Why use Calendar Maker

Building a calendar by hand in Canva means drawing a table and typing every date — easy to get wrong, especially across months. Calendar Maker generates the correct dates for any month, year, or range automatically, then hands you an editable Canva design to customize.
