**Calendar Maker** is a Canva app that generates a ready-to-edit calendar — monthly, weekly, or for any custom date range — and drops it straight into your design. No drawing tables, no typing dates by hand. This guide covers **every feature**, step by step, with screenshots.

## Install and open Calendar Maker

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Apps panel in Canva">
Open any design, then select **Apps** in the left toolbar.
</Step>
<Step title="Search and launch">
Search for **Calendar Maker** and open it. The app loads in the side panel.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a calendar type">
At the top you'll see three tabs — **Month**, **Week**, and **Custom**. Each builds a calendar a different way.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Tab 1 — Month (monthly calendars)

The Month tab builds the familiar month grid (weekdays across the top, dates in the cells).

<Frame caption="Calendar Maker — the Month tab">
<img src="/images/calendar-maker/calendar-maker-month-tab-panel.png" alt="Calendar Maker Month tab showing month and year dropdowns, page templates, and Add to design" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose month and year">
Pick the **month** and **year** (10 years before to 10 years after the current year).
</Step>
<Step title="Add all 12 months (optional)">
Turn on **Add all months** to generate the whole year at once. Turn on **Each month on its own page** to place every month on a separate, auto-named page.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the week start">
Toggle **Start week with Sunday** for the US layout (the app guesses your default by region).
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a page template (optional)">
Choose a decorated **page template** to place the calendar on a designed background.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Tab 2 — Week (weekly planners & timetables)

The Week tab builds a table by weekday — great for class schedules and weekly planners.

<Frame caption="The Week tab — choose weekdays and rows">
<img src="/images/calendar-maker/calendar-maker-week-tab-days.png" alt="Calendar Maker Week tab with weekday checkboxes and number of rows" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick which days appear">
Check the weekdays you want (at least one). Use **Start week with Sunday** to move Sunday first.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the number of rows">
Set **Number of rows** (1–27) to add blank rows under each day for notes or plans.
</Step>
<Step title="Shorten labels (optional)">
Turn on **Shorten labels** to abbreviate day names (e.g. "Monday" → "Mon").
</Step>
</Steps>

## Tab 3 — Custom (any date range)

The Custom tab builds a calendar for any span of dates — a school term, a project timeline, a season.

<Frame caption="The Custom tab — pick a start and end date">
<img src="/images/calendar-maker/calendar-maker-custom-tab-date-range.png" alt="Calendar Maker Custom tab with start date and end date fields" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
<Step title="Set start and end dates">
Choose a **Start date** and **End date**. The maximum span is **150 days** — beyond that the app shows an error on the date field.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the week start">
Use **Start week with Sunday** as in the other tabs.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Styling: fonts, sizes, and alignment

Every tab has style controls grouped in collapsible sections, with a **live preview** that updates as you adjust.

## Page templates (decorated layouts)

On the **Month** tab you can place the calendar on a pre-designed page (about 14 themes such as Botanical, Watercolor, Scandi Minimal, Midcentury, Coastal, Vintage Postcard). The app fills in the month and year and matches the calendar's colors to the template.

<Note>
Design sizes may differ from the template, so you may need to nudge elements to line up after adding.
</Note>

## Save and reuse your setups

Below **Add to design** there's a **Save settings after adding successfully** checkbox. When it's on, your full configuration is saved each time you add a calendar. Each tab keeps its own **Your saved setups** carousel — tap a saved card to restore everything (dates, fonts, colors, template). The app keeps your ~30 most recent setups per tab.

## Add the calendar to your design

<Steps>
<Step title="Click Add to design">
When you're happy with the preview, click **Add to design**. The calendar (and a new page, if you used a template or multiple months) drops into your Canva design.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep editing in Canva">
Select the calendar to keep adjusting its style directly in Canva.
</Step>
<Step title="Download or print">
Download as PDF Print for the cleanest result, then print at home or order prints.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Free vs Pro

Pro users see the **Add to design** button and full access. Without Pro, you'll see **Unlock to use** (opens an upgrade page) and **Restart app** (re-checks your status after unlocking). The app checks your Pro status automatically through your Canva account.

## Languages

Calendar Maker is multilingual — month names, weekday names, and the interface follow your language, and the default week start (Monday or Sunday) is guessed from your region.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="How do I make a printable 2026 calendar?">
On the Month tab, set the year to 2026, generate each month (or use **Add all months**), add your holidays and notes, then download as PDF Print.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I make a calendar for a custom date range?">
Yes — use the **Custom** tab and set a start and end date (up to 150 days).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I reuse my colors and fonts next time?">
Yes — turn on **Save settings after adding successfully**, then restore from **Your saved setups** on each tab.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why don't I see the Add to design button?">
That button is for Pro users. If you see **Unlock to use**, upgrade, then click **Restart app** to refresh your status.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
