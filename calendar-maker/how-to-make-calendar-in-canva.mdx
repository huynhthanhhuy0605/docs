You can build a calendar in Canva from scratch with a table, but generating one with the [Calendar Maker](/calendar-maker/what-is-calendar-maker) app is faster and gets every date right. This guide shows you how to create a monthly, weekly, or printable calendar — including a printable 2026 calendar — then customize and print it.

## Make a calendar in Canva with Calendar Maker

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Calendar Maker in Canva">
From the **Apps** panel, open Calendar Maker.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter the month and year">
Type the month and year you want, or choose a **custom date range** for a non-standard calendar.
</Step>

<Step title="Generate the calendar">
Generate the grid — Calendar Maker fills in the correct dates and weekday layout automatically.
</Step>

<Step title="Customize the design">
Add it to your canvas, then style the colors, fonts, headings, and images with Canva's tools.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Open Calendar Maker in Canva" icon="calendar" href="https://qrdynamic.xyz/calendarmaker-in-canva" horizontal>
Generate a calendar for any month, year, or date range in seconds.
</Card>

## Choose any month or a custom date range

Calendar Maker isn't limited to full calendar months. Set a custom start and end date to build a calendar for a school term, a project timeline, or a specific season — useful when a standard monthly grid doesn't fit.

## Make a printable 2026 calendar

To create a printable monthly calendar for 2026:

<Steps>
<Step title="Generate each month for 2026">
Enter **2026** and the month you need, then generate. Repeat for each month you want to print.
</Step>

<Step title="Add holidays and notes">
Type in holidays, birthdays, or reminders directly on the calendar.
</Step>

<Step title="Set up for printing">
Choose a print size (A4 or US Letter is standard), and keep margins inside the printable area.
</Step>

<Step title="Download as PDF and print">
Download as **PDF Print** for the cleanest result, then print at home or send to a print shop.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Demand for printable calendars peaks from November through January. If you're publishing calendar designs, prepare your next-year templates before Q4.
</Tip>
