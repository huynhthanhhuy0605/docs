Store-bought paint-by-numbers kits use stock pictures. With [QrdyCraft](/qrdy-craft/what-is-qrdycraft) you can turn *your own* photo — your kid, the family dog, a holiday snap — into a custom, number-guided craft in seconds. Technically QrdyCraft builds a **mosaic sticker-by-number** (a paint-by-numbers-style game where each numbered piece maps to a color), which your child can complete on screen or print and stick at home.

This guide walks through the generator step by step.

## Turn a photo into paint by numbers

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the QrdyCraft generator">
Go to [qrdyai.com/craft](https://qrdyai.com/craft) and open the create page. No app or sign-in is needed to start.
</Step>

<Step title="Upload your photo (or type a keyword)">
Upload a picture, or just type a keyword like "rainbow butterfly" if you don't have a photo ready. Simple images with a clear subject trace best.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick a difficulty">
Choose how many pieces the mosaic breaks into. More pieces means a harder, longer craft — match it to your child's age (see the table below).
</Step>

<Step title="Let the generator build the mosaic">
In a few seconds, QrdyCraft's AI traces your image into a numbered, low-poly mosaic of colored pieces — the paint-by-numbers layout.
</Step>

<Step title="Play on screen or print">
Play online by dragging numbered pieces into place, or download a print-ready PDF to complete by hand at home.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Match the difficulty to your child's age

The generator scales the number of pieces so the craft isn't too easy or too frustrating:

| Difficulty | Pieces | Suggested age |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 30–50 | 4–6 |
| Medium | 60–100 | 7–9 |
| Hard | 120–200 | 10–12 |

<Tip>
Photos with a single clear subject and good contrast trace into cleaner paint-by-numbers than busy group shots. If the first result looks noisy, try a simpler picture or a lower difficulty.
</Tip>

## A note on your photos

Uploaded photos are only traced for their outline and then discarded — your original image isn't stored, and paint-by-numbers made from uploaded photos never appear in the public gallery. Only keyword-based creations can become public.
