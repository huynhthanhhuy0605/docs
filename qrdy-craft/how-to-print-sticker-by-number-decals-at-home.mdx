Want a real, peel-and-stick craft your kids can finish at the kitchen table? **QrdyCraft** turns any photo — or even just a keyword like "rainbow butterfly" — into a **mosaic sticker-by-number**, then gives you two print-ready PDFs. This guide walks through the whole print path: generate the mosaic, download the files, get the decals printed and kiss-cut, and set your child up to stick by number.

QrdyCraft is **parent-operated, kid-played**: you create and print it, your child (roughly ages 4–12) does the sticking. The whole print run usually costs about $1–3 per sheet at a local decal shop.

## Before you start

You'll need a QrdyAI account (free to start — the first creation is free) and access to a **print-and-cut decal shop**. That's any print shop with a print-and-cut machine such as a Roland VersaSTUDIO or Mimaki CJV. Many local sign, sticker, and copy shops have one.

<Note>
Prefer no printing at all? Every mosaic can also be played online — share a link or QR code and your child drags numbered stickers into place on a phone or tablet. This guide covers the **print** path; the online path needs no setup beyond sharing the link.
</Note>

## Step 1 — Generate your mosaic

</Step>

<Step title="Add a photo or a keyword">
Type a keyword (like *rainbow butterfly*) for a gallery-friendly design, or switch to the **Upload photo** tab to use your own picture — your kid, the family dog, a drawing. Uploaded photos stay private and are never published to the public gallery.

<Step title="Generate">
Click **Generate**. In a few seconds you'll see the finished color mosaic. You can preview it as **Mosaic art**, **Numbered grid**, **Sticker sheet**, or **Color legend**.

## Step 2 — Download the two PDFs

Click **Download**. QrdyCraft gives you a ZIP with two files, each for a different purpose:

- **`mosaic-preview.pdf`** (5 pages, for your family): cover, finished art, numbered grid, sticker sheet, and color key.
- **`stickers-print-and-cut.pdf`** (1 page, for the print shop): the sticker sheet with a special **CutContour** line so a print-and-cut machine knows exactly where to kiss-cut each decal.

<Note>
On the free plan, a small `Made with QrdyCraft` watermark appears only on the cover and finished-art pages. The **numbered grid, sticker sheet, and color key — the pages your child actually uses — never carry a watermark**, and on the print-and-cut file the watermark sits outside the cut line, so the machine trims it away.
</Note>

## Step 3 — Get the decals printed and cut

Take **`stickers-print-and-cut.pdf`** to a decal print shop and ask for:

> "A4, transparent PVC decal, print and kiss-cut on the CutContour spot color. One sheet."

That "kiss-cut" instruction tells the machine to cut through the sticker layer but not the backing, so each numbered piece peels off cleanly. This usually takes 5–15 minutes and costs about **$1–3 per sheet**.

<Warning>
Print results, materials, machine compatibility, pricing, and turnaround vary by shop. **Always confirm the file, material, cut line, and price with your print shop before you order.** QrdyCraft and QRdy are not responsible for print quality, cutting accuracy, or any costs from a third-party print service. When possible, ask for a single test sheet first.
</Warning>

## Step 4 — Print the reference pages at home

From **`mosaic-preview.pdf`**, print **pages 3–5** on plain A4 paper:

- **Page 3 — numbered grid:** the board your child sticks onto.
- **Page 4 — sticker sheet:** shows where each numbered piece goes.
- **Page 5 — color key:** matches numbers to colors.

No special paper or printer needed here — a home inkjet or laser printer is fine.

## Step 5 — Peel and stick by number

Give your child the printed numbered grid and the cut decal sheet. They **peel a sticker, find the grid cell with the same number, and press it down**. For the first few seconds each decal can still slide, so a slightly crooked piece is easy to nudge straight. When the grid is full, the picture appears.

## Tips and troubleshooting

Save your project so you can reprint later — every mosaic lives in **My Creations**, where you can re-download the PDFs, tweak difficulty or palette and re-tessellate, or share a play-online link. On the free plan you can keep up to 5 projects (older ones are hidden, not deleted); Pro removes the limit.

If a print shop asks about the extra line in the file, explain it's a **spot color named CutContour** used to drive the cutter — they'll recognize it. If your shop can't do transparent PVC, matte white sticker vinyl works too; the decals just won't be see-through.

## Do it in Canva instead

QrdyCraft also runs as a **Canva app**. Inside Canva you can generate a mosaic and add it straight to your design as a PNG. Note that **PDF download isn't available in the Canva version yet** — for the print-and-cut decal files, use the web app at [qrdyai.com/craft](https://qrdyai.com/craft).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Do I need a special printer at home?">
No. The at-home pages (numbered grid, sticker sheet, color key) print fine on any inkjet or laser printer. Only the decal sheet needs a print-and-cut machine, which is why you take that one file to a print shop.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How much does printing the decals cost?">
Typically about $1–3 per A4 sheet at a local decal shop, and it usually takes 5–15 minutes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are my uploaded photos kept private?">
Yes. QrdyCraft traces only the outline of an uploaded photo and discards the original, and mosaics made from uploads are never added to the public gallery. Only keyword-based designs can appear there.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What age is this good for?">
Roughly 4–12. Match the difficulty to the child: Easy (30–50 pieces) for ages 4–6, Medium (60–100) for 7–9, and Hard (120–200) for 10–12.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
