Want a paint-by-numbers activity your kids can do right now, without a printer or a trip to the print shop? **QrdyCraft** turns any photo — or even just a keyword like "rainbow butterfly" — into a **mosaic sticker-by-number** your child can play entirely online: they drag numbered stickers into the matching cells until the picture appears. This guide covers the no-printing path from start to finish.

QrdyCraft is **parent-operated, kid-played**: you create the mosaic, then hand it off (a link or QR code) so your child, roughly ages 4–12, does the actual playing on a phone or tablet.

## Before you start

All you need is a free QrdyAI account (the first creation is free) and a device to play on. There's **no printing, no print shop, and no special materials** — the whole thing runs in the browser.

## Step 1 — Make your paint-by-numbers mosaic

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Create page">
Go to [qrdyai.com/craft](https://qrdyai.com/craft) and click **Create yours — free**. If you're not signed in, QrdyCraft sends you to sign in and brings you back.
</Step>

<Step title="Add a photo or a keyword">
Type a keyword (like *rainbow butterfly*) for a gallery-friendly design, or switch to the **Upload photo** tab to use your own picture — your kid, the family dog, a drawing. Uploaded photos stay private and are never published to the public gallery.
</Step>

<Step title="Generate">
Click **Generate**. In a few seconds you'll see the finished color mosaic. You can preview it as **Mosaic art**, **Numbered grid**, **Sticker sheet**, or **Color legend**.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick difficulty and palette">
Match the difficulty to your child's age, then choose a palette (Pastel, Vibrant, Ocean, Forest, Rainbow, Candy) and click **Re-tessellate** to rebuild the pieces on the same picture. For a first try, **Medium · Rainbow** keeps a 6-year-old busy for about 30 minutes.

| Difficulty | Sticker pieces | Suggested age |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 30–50 | 4–6 |
| Medium | 60–100 | 7–9 |
| Hard | 120–200 | 10–12 |
</Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2 — Start playing online

Once your mosaic is ready, click **Play online**. You can also reach the play screen later from **My Creations** or from any design in the public **gallery**.

The play screen looks like a game board: a title, a **progress bar** (for example, "38 / 48"), the numbered mosaic grid, and a tray of round numbered stickers along the bottom.

<Steps>
<Step title="Match the numbers">
Your child drags a sticker from the tray to the grid cell that shows the **same number**. Each correct placement bumps the progress bar up.
</Step>

<Step title="Fix mistakes easily">
Pieces snap into their cells, so it's forgiving — a wrong number simply won't lock into the wrong spot.
</Step>

<Step title="Finish the picture">
When the last sticker is placed, the puzzle celebrates with **confetti** and reveals the full-color image.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Progress is saved on the device as you go (and on the server if you're the owner), so a half-finished mosaic is still there when your child comes back to it.
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Do I need to print anything to play?">
No. The play-online path runs entirely in the browser — generate a mosaic, open **Play online**, and drag numbered stickers into place. Printing is only for the separate peel-and-stick decal path.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is this a paint-by-numbers maker for my own photos?">
Yes. Upload a photo and QrdyCraft turns it into a numbered mosaic you can play online. Uploaded photos are traced for their outline, the original is discarded, and those designs never appear in the public gallery.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What device is best for playing?">
A phone or tablet works well for dragging stickers, but any browser is fine. Share the play link or QR code to open the game on your child's device.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What age is this good for?">
Roughly 4–12. Match the difficulty to the child: Easy (30–50 pieces) for ages 4–6, Medium (60–100) for 7–9, and Hard (120–200) for 10–12.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
