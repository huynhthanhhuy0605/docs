**QrdyCraft** turns a photo — or just a keyword — into a **mosaic sticker-by-number**. It's **parent-operated and kid-played**: you set it up, your child (ages 4–12) finishes it on a screen or printed. QrdyCraft runs on the **web** ([qrdyai.com/craft](https://qrdyai.com/craft)) and as a **Canva app**. This guide covers **every feature**, step by step, with screenshots.

## How it works (the short version)

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a photo or keyword">
Upload a picture or type a keyword like "rainbow butterfly".
</Step>
<Step title="AI builds the mosaic">
In a few seconds you get a numbered, colored mosaic.
</Step>
<Step title="Play or print">
Play online by dragging numbered stickers, or download a print-ready PDF.
</Step>
<Step title="Complete the picture">
Match each numbered piece to reveal the finished art.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Difficulty and age

Difficulty sets the number of pieces, which maps to age:

| Difficulty | Pieces | Suggested age |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 30–50 | 4–6 |
| Medium | 60–100 | 7–9 |
| Hard | 120–200 | 10–12 |

For a first try, **Medium · Rainbow** keeps a 6-year-old busy for about 30 minutes.

## On the web (qrdyai.com/craft)

### Create a mosaic

<Frame caption="The web create page — describe with text or upload an image">
<img src="/images/qrdy-craft/web/qrdy-craft-web-create-page.png" alt="QrdyCraft create page with Describe with text and Upload image tabs and a Generate button" />

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose your input">
On **Create**, type a keyword (default) or switch to **Upload image** (PNG/JPEG/WebP, up to 5MB).
</Step>
<Step title="Generate">
Click **Generate**. The app traces and tessellates the image and shows the finished color mosaic.
</Step>
<Step title="Tweak difficulty and palette">
Change the **difficulty** and **palette** (Pastel, Vibrant, Ocean, Forest, Rainbow, Candy), then **Re-tessellate** to rebuild while keeping the same picture.
</Step>
<Step title="View the modes">
See it as **Mosaic art**, **Numbered grid**, **Sticker sheet**, and **Color legend**.
</Step>
<Step title="Download">
Download a ZIP with two PDFs (see Print & stick below).
</Step>
</Steps>

### Print &amp; stick at home

The downloaded ZIP has two PDFs:

- **`mosaic-preview.pdf`** (5 pages, for the family): cover, finished art, numbered grid, sticker sheet, color key.
- **`stickers-print-and-cut.pdf`** (1 page, for a print shop): includes a special **CutContour** line so a print-and-cut machine kiss-cuts the decals.

Take the print-and-cut PDF to a **decal print shop** (Roland VersaSTUDIO / Mimaki CJV): ask for "A4, transparent PVC decal, print and kiss-cut on the CutContour spot color." Print pages 3–5 of the preview at home, and your child peels and sticks by number.

## Privacy

Uploaded photos are only **traced for their outline and then discarded** — the original isn't stored, and mosaics from uploaded photos never appear in the public gallery. Only keyword-based creations can become public.

## Free vs Pro

Free includes a few AI (keyword) creations and 1 photo upload per month, all difficulties and palettes, the gallery, and print-ready PDFs. The **numbered grid, sticker sheet, and color key your child uses are always watermark-free**; the small watermark only appears on the cover and finished-art pages. Pro ($9.99/month or $79/year) removes the limits and the watermark, and adds priority processing.
