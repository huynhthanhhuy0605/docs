QrdyCraft turns any photo — or even just a keyword — into a **mosaic sticker-by-number** that a child can finish in two ways: play it on a screen by dragging numbered stickers into place, or print it and stick real decals by number at home. You set it up, your kids enjoy the result.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a photo or a keyword">
Upload a picture (your kid, the family dog, a drawing) or just type a keyword like "rainbow butterfly".
</Step>

<Step title="AI builds the mosaic">
In a few seconds, the image becomes a numbered, low-poly mosaic made of colored pieces.
</Step>

<Step title="Play on screen or print">
Play online by dragging numbered stickers into place, or download a print-ready PDF.
</Step>

<Step title="Complete it piece by piece">
Match each numbered sticker to its spot to reveal the finished, full-color picture.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Who it's for

QrdyCraft is **parent-operated and kid-played**: you (the parent) type the keyword, pick the difficulty, and download or share the result; your child (roughly ages 4–12) plays it. Difficulty scales with age:

| Difficulty | Pieces | Suggested age |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 30–50 | 4–6 |
| Medium | 60–100 | 7–9 |
| Hard | 120–200 | 10–12 |

## Web and Canva

QrdyCraft runs on the web at [qrdyai.com/craft](https://qrdyai.com/craft) — the full experience with online play, print-ready PDFs, a gallery, and your saved creations. It also runs as a **Canva app**, where you create a mosaic and add it straight into your design as an image.

## Privacy

Uploaded photos are only traced for their outline and then discarded — the original image is not stored, and mosaics made from uploaded photos never appear in the public gallery. Only keyword-based creations can become public.
