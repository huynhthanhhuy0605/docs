When you need a frame in *your* shape — a logo, a grid, an arc, or even your own letters — you need a frame app. Here are the **best Canva apps for frames in 2026**, organized by the job you're trying to do, so you can jump straight to the right one.

## 1. Image to Frames — turn any image into a frame

If you have a shape — a logo, a star, a silhouette — **[Image to Frames](/image-to-frames/what-is-image-to-frames)** converts it into a real Canva frame you can drop photos or videos into. It supports PNG, SVG, and JPG (up to 5MB), follows the image's true outline when the background is transparent, and adds an optional border.

</Frame>

**Best for:** turning an existing graphic or logo into a custom frame.
**Tip:** remove the background first, or the frame comes out as a rectangle.

## 2. Frames Lab — build custom frame shapes

When you need a frame built to spec, **[Frames Lab](/frames-lab/frames-lab-complete-guide)** is the toolbox. It creates frames in five modes — **Shape, Burst, Grid, Arc, and Diagonal** — with live controls (grid columns/rows, arc sweep angle, burst density), plus a library of ready-made frames in **Explore** and effects (blur, border, inner shadow) in **Style**.

## 3. Text to Frames — fill your text with photos

**[Text to Frames](/text-to-frames/text-to-frames-complete-guide)** turns each letter of a word into its own frame, so you can drop a *different* photo or video into every letter — something a single clipping mask can't do. It includes 100+ fonts and three effects (blur, border, inner shadow).

## 4. Trace to Frame — draw a frame by hand

**Trace to Frame** lets you place points to draw a frame with straight lines, cropping a photo to any custom shape you outline. It's the manual, full-control option when you want to trace around a specific object.

**Best for:** tracing a custom outline by hand (straight-line shapes).

## 5. Frame Collage — photo collage grids

**Frame Collage** builds a grid collage — toggle cells from 8×8 up to 32×32 — where every cell is a frame you fill with a photo or video. Great for mosaics and multi-photo layouts.

<Frame caption="Frame Collage turns a grid into fillable cells for a multi-photo layout">
<img src="/images/guides/frame-grid.png" alt="A photo split across a 3x3 grid of frame cells made with Frame Collage" />
</Frame>

**Best for:** quick collage grids with many photo slots.

## 6. AI Frames — describe it, AI draws it

**AI Frames** generates a frame from a text description, plus a library of 20,000+ free frames and exclusive shadow/border/effects. The easiest option when you don't have a source image and don't want to build a shape by hand.

**Best for:** getting a frame from words, or browsing a big ready-made library.

## 7. Clipping Mask — one image through all your text

Not technically a frame app, but closely related: **Clipping Mask** shows a single image through your text or shape at once (versus Text to Frames, where each letter is a separate frame). Reach for it when you want one photo spanning a whole word.

**Best for:** one image visible through an entire word or shape.
