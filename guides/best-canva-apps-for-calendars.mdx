A calendar app does the counting for you — pick a month, and the grid comes out correct. ## 1. Calendar Maker — best overall calendar generator

[Calendar Maker](/calendar-maker/what-is-calendar-maker) is the app to reach for when you want a *correct* calendar without building a table. Enter a month and year — or any custom date range — and it generates the grid and inserts it straight into your open Canva design. It covers three modes from a single panel:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Month">
    A familiar month grid. Generate one month, or tick **Add all months** to build all twelve at once — optionally one month per page. Choose whether the week starts on Sunday or Monday (it guesses from your region).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Week">
    A weekday table for schedules and weekly planners. Pick which days appear and set the number of rows (1–27) so each day has blank space for notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Custom">
    A calendar for any date range up to 150 days — handy for a term, a trip, or a sprint that doesn't line up with a calendar month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every mode is fully styleable: font, bold/italic/underline, alignment, size and color for both the weekday headers and the date numbers, plus header and alternating-row background colors. There are 30+ ready-made color palettes and about 14 decorative page templates (Botanical, Watercolor, Scandi Minimal, Coastal, and more) if you'd rather start from a designed page. A live preview shows your styling before you insert, and you can save setups to reuse later.

**Best for:** printable 2026 calendars, content calendars, class schedules, and anyone who makes calendars regularly and wants every date right the first time. Calendar Maker's full calendar features are a Pro feature.

## 2. Canva's built-in calendar templates — best zero-setup option

Don't overlook Canva itself. Search "calendar" in the Templates panel and you'll find hundreds of pre-designed monthly and yearly layouts you can edit in place. There's no counting or generating — you pick a look and swap the text.

The honest trade-off: templates are a *fixed year and layout*. If you need a specific month, a custom date range, or the dates to be generated correctly rather than typed by hand, you'll be editing a lot of cells manually — which is exactly the work a generator like Calendar Maker removes. For a one-off, good-looking calendar where the exact dates matter less, the built-in templates are the fastest path.

**Best for:** a quick, attractive calendar when you don't need a specific or custom date range.

## 3. Frames Lab — best for decorating around a calendar

A calendar grid is only half a wall or desk calendar; the border sells it. [Frames Lab](/frames-lab/how-to-make-custom-frames-in-canva) builds custom frame shapes and decorative borders — arcs, grids, bursts, diagonals, and traced shapes — that you can wrap around a generated calendar to finish the page. Pair it with Calendar Maker: generate the grid, then frame it.

**Best for:** wall and desk calendars, planner covers, and any calendar page that needs a designed border rather than a plain grid.

## 4. Image to Frames — best for photo calendars

A photo calendar is really twelve framed photos plus twelve month grids. [Image to Frames](/image-to-frames/what-is-image-to-frames) turns any image into a Canva frame, so you can crop each month's photo into a clean shape with a border, then drop a generated grid beneath it. Build all twelve months with Calendar Maker's **Add all months** option and you have a full photo calendar without hand-cropping a single picture.

**Best for:** family photo calendars, brand calendars, and gift calendars where each month features a different image.

## How to choose

If you make calendars with any regularity, start with **Calendar Maker** — it's the only option here that generates correct dates for any month, week, or custom range and inserts them straight into Canva. Reach for **Canva's built-in templates** when you just need one good-looking calendar fast and the exact dates aren't critical. Add **Frames Lab** when the page needs a decorative border, and **Image to Frames** when you're building a photo calendar. The three Qrdy apps are designed to stack: generate, frame, decorate.

## FAQ

**Is there a free way to make a calendar in Canva?**
Yes — Canva's built-in calendar templates are free to edit, and you can also build a calendar manually with a table. Calendar Maker's full generation features are a Pro feature; it saves the manual work of typing and aligning every date.

**What's the fastest way to make a printable 2026 calendar?**
Use Calendar Maker's **Add all months** option to generate all twelve months at once (optionally one per page), style them, then export to PDF for print. See [How to Make a Calendar in Canva](/calendar-maker/how-to-make-calendar-in-canva).

**Can I make a weekly planner instead of a month grid?**
Yes. Calendar Maker's **Week** tab builds a weekday table with adjustable rows for notes — ideal for a weekly schedule or planner.
