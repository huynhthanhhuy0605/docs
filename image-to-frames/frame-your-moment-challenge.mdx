Frame Your Moment is a creative challenge built around [Image to Frames](/image-to-frames/what-is-image-to-frames). You turn a single photo into framed art inside Canva, share your before & after on social media, and the entries with the most views win prizes you can't buy anywhere.

This page explains what the challenge is, what you can win, and the rules. There's nothing to submit here — see [Taking part](#taking-part) at the end.

## The prize: a license money can't buy

The headline prize is a **Lifetime License to Image to Frames** — never sold, never subscribed. Only **three exist**, each individually numbered, and winning is the only way to own one.

| Place | Prize |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | Lifetime License **LT-001** + **$100 cash** |
| 2nd | Lifetime License **LT-002** + **$50 cash** |
| 3rd | Lifetime License **LT-003** + **$50 cash** |

<Info>
Everyone who joins gets **1 month of full Image to Frames access** — whether or not you place in the top three.
</Info>

## How the challenge works

<Steps>
<Step title="Join free">
Sign in to unlock 1 month of full Image to Frames access.
</Step>

<Step title="Make your art">
Turn one photo into framed art with Image to Frames — no design skills needed.
</Step>

<Step title="Film your before & after">
Record the transformation, or let the built-in Video Maker render a clean 9:16 before & after for you.
</Step>

<Step title="Post & enter">
Share your clip publicly on TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts with **#FrameYourMoment #ImageToFrames**. The posts with the most views win.
</Step>
</Steps>

## How winners are picked

Winners are ranked purely by **views on the tagged public post**. The three entries with the most views by the deadline take LT-001, LT-002, and LT-003 in rank order. View counts are read from the public post and refreshed twice a week.

## Key dates

- **Entries close:** July 20, 2026 at 23:59 UTC
- **Winners announced:** July 27, 2026
</Note>

## Rules

<Accordion title="How to enter">
