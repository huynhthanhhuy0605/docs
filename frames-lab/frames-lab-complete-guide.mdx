**Frames Lab** is a Canva app for building frames exactly the way you want — grids, arcs, sunbursts, diagonal layouts, or a frame from your own shape — then dropping any photo or video inside. Frames auto-fit your design, so you never resize by hand. This guide covers **every feature**, step by step, with screenshots.

### Shape — turn a shape into a frame

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a shape">
From **Elements → Shapes** or **Graphics**, add a shape to your design.
</Step>
<Step title="Recheck design">
Click **Recheck design** so the app detects it.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the frame">
Click the detected frame to place it. Set a **border** first if you want one.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Whenever you change a shape in your design, click **Recheck design** again to update.
</Tip>

### Burst — random sunburst frames

<Frame caption="Burst mode — random spark/sunburst frames">
<img src="/images/frames-lab/frames-lab-create-burst-mode.png" alt="Frames Lab Burst mode with random preview and style controls" />

Burst generates random sunburst/spark frames. Tune them with the controls, and grab one you like — results are random, so each change makes new previews.

<Frame caption="Burst style options">
<img src="/images/frames-lab/frames-lab-burst-style-options.png" alt="Burst style dropdown with Bubble, Splash, Radial, Droplet, Spark, Leaf" />

<Frame caption="The full set of Burst controls">

Controls: **Style** (Spark, Bubble, Splash, Radial, Droplet, Leaf…), **Density**, **Size**, **Spread**, **Variation** (Clean/Natural/Wild), **Distribution** (Burst/Ring/Spiral/Scatter), and **Preview items**.

### Grid — collage frames

<Frame caption="Grid mode — dimensions and layout">
<img src="/images/frames-lab/frames-lab-create-grid-mode.png" alt="Frames Lab Grid mode with grid dimensions and column/row/gap controls" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
<Step title="Set dimensions">
Adjust the grid **width** and **height**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the layout">
Choose **columns, rows, and gap**. The preview updates live.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a wave and advanced options">
Open **Wave effect** to curve the lines, or **Advanced options** for combined cells and corner radius.
</Step>
<Step title="Add to design">
Click **Add to design**, then drop a photo into each cell.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Grid wave effect and advanced options">
<img src="/images/frames-lab/frames-lab-grid-advanced-wave.png" alt="Grid wave effect with speed and height, plus combine cells and corner radius" />
</Frame>

<Note>
Very complex grids (too many cells or a strong wave) get blocked — reduce the rows/columns or wave strength.
</Note>

### Arc — curved frames

<Frame caption="Arc mode — set the sweep angle">
<img src="/images/frames-lab/frames-lab-create-arc-mode.png" alt="Frames Lab Arc mode with sweep angle control" />
</Frame>

Set the **sweep angle** (how much of the arc, in degrees) and the **start angle**, add a border if you want, and click **Add to design**.

### Diagonal — angled grid

<Frame caption="Diagonal mode — strips on an angle">
<img src="/images/frames-lab/frames-lab-create-diagonal-mode.png" alt="Frames Lab Diagonal mode preview with diagonal strips" />
</Frame>

Like Grid, but the strips run on an angle you control (spacing, gap, and angle), plus the same wave and advanced options.

## Explore tab — ready-made frames

<Frame caption="Explore — themed frame collections">
<img src="/images/frames-lab/frames-lab-explore-collections.png" alt="Explore tab with themed frame collections like Trending and seasonal sets" />
</Frame>

Open **Explore** and click any frame from the themed collections (Trending, seasonal sets, and more) to drop it straight into your design — no setup needed.

## Style tab — add effects

<Frame caption="Style — blur, border, and inner shadow on your last frames">
<img src="/images/frames-lab/frames-lab-style-tab-effects.png" alt="Style tab with Last frames and blur, border, inner shadow controls" />
</Frame>

Every frame you add is saved under **Last frames** in the **Style** tab. Set **Blur**, **Border**, and/or **Inner shadow** (each with a color and strength), then click a frame to re-add it with the effects applied.

## Drop in your photo

After any frame is in your design, drag a photo or video onto it — it crops to the frame's shape automatically. Double-click to reposition inside.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Why doesn't my shape show up as a frame?">
Add the shape from **Elements → Shapes/Graphics**, then click **Recheck design**.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="My grid or diagonal won't add">
It's too complex — reduce rows/columns or the wave strength and try again.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="My burst looks different every time">
Burst is fully random. When you see one you like, add it right away — changing a control regenerates the previews.
</Accordion>
