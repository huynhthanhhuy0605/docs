Want to drop a photo inside a star, a heart, or any custom shape in Canva? Canva's built-in frames only come in a handful of basic shapes, so there's no obvious way to *fill your own shape* with an image. The fix is **Frames Lab**, a Canva app that turns any shape already in your design into a frame — then you drag a photo in and it crops to that exact outline. The frame auto-fits your design, so you never resize by hand.

## What you'll need

Frames Lab runs in the sidebar inside the Canva editor. Before you start, open your design and install the app from the Canva Apps menu. The free tier lets you add a limited number of frames; Pro removes the limit.

<Note>
Frames Lab turns a shape into a real frame — a container you drop photos or videos into. That's different from simply recoloring a shape. Once it's a frame, your image crops to the shape automatically.
</Note>

## How to fill a shape with a picture

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a shape to your design">
In Canva, open **Elements → Shapes** (or **Elements → Graphics**) and click the shape you want to fill — a circle, star, heart, blob, or any graphic. You can also use a shape made with Canva's AI Shape tool. Place it on your page at roughly the size you want.
</Step>

<Step title="Open Frames Lab and choose Shape mode">
Open the **Frames Lab** app from the sidebar. On the **Create** tab, pick **Shape** from the Create mode strip at the top. This is the mode that turns existing shapes into frames.
</Step>

<Step title="Click Recheck design">
Click **Recheck design**. Frames Lab scans your page and lists the shapes and images it recognizes under **Frames**. If nothing appears, make sure the shape came from **Elements → Shapes/Graphics**, then click **Recheck design** again.
</Step>

<Step title="Add a border (optional)">
Want an outline around your photo? Open the **Border** section and choose a color and thickness *before* you add the frame.
</Step>

<Step title="Click the frame to add it">
Click the shape in the **Frames** list. Frames Lab drops a frame in that exact shape onto your design, sized to fit.
</Step>

<Step title="Drop in your picture">
Drag any photo or video onto the frame. The image snaps to the shape and crops automatically. Double-click it to reposition the picture inside the frame.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Every time you change the shape in your design, click **Recheck design** again so Frames Lab picks up the latest version.
</Tip>

## Add a photo border to your shape

A border turns a plain photo-shape into a finished graphic. In Shape mode, open the **Border** section, pick a color, and set the thickness before clicking the frame. The outline hugs the shape's edge — handy for badges, stickers, and profile cutouts.

Already added the frame and want more polish? Switch to the **Style** tab. Every frame you add is saved there under **Last frames**, where you can re-add it with three extra effects:

## Don't want to build a shape yourself?

If you just need a good-looking shaped frame fast, skip the shape step and open the **Explore** tab instead. It loads ready-made frame collections grouped by theme — click any one to drop it straight into your design, then add your photo. No setup required.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="How do I fill a shape with a picture in Canva?">
Add a shape from **Elements → Shapes/Graphics**, open Frames Lab, choose **Shape** mode on the Create tab, click **Recheck design**, then click the shape to turn it into a frame. Drag a photo onto the frame and it crops to that shape.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I turn any shape into a frame?">
Yes. Frames Lab recognizes shapes and graphics added from **Elements → Shapes/Graphics**, including shapes made with Canva's AI Shape tool. Click **Recheck design** so the app detects it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why doesn't my shape show up as a frame?">
Make sure the shape was added from **Elements → Shapes/Graphics**, then click **Recheck design** so the app picks it up. Re-run Recheck design any time you edit the design.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I change the shape of a picture I already added?">
Make the shape you want, turn it into a frame with Shape mode, then drag your existing picture onto the new frame. The image re-crops to the new shape automatically.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is Frames Lab free?">
Frames Lab has a free tier with a limit on how many frames you can add. Pro removes the limit.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
